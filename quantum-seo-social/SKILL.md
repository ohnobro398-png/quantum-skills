# Quantum SEO Social

## Win Facebook search

- Put the exact keyword customers type, such as Self Care Combo, in the first line of every caption. Facebook search weighs the opening line heaviest.

- Repeat the keyword naturally 2 to 3 times in the post: first line, one benefit line, and the hashtags. Never stuff it into unreadable repetition.

- Keep the Page username, Page name and about section keyword rich, so the Page itself surfaces when people search the product category.

- Use 5 to 8 hashtags that mix the exact product keyword, the shop name and one or two broad shopping tags. Drop tags that never bring views after 2 weeks.

- Check weekly which search terms bring profile visits in Page insights, and shift next week's keywords toward the winners.

## Rank web tools on Google

- Give every tool page one clear keyword in the title tag, the H1 and the first paragraph. One page targets one keyword.

- Write meta descriptions as a promise of the outcome, under 160 characters, in the user's language.

- Add FAQ schema with real questions users ask, since tool pages win featured snippets through direct answers.

- Keep pages fast: one font pair, images sized to display size, no render blocking scripts above the fold.

- Build one page per tool or topic instead of cramming many keywords into one page. Focused pages rank, diluted pages do not.

## Persuade ethically

- Lead with social proof: real customer counts, real reviews, real photos. Specific proof beats adjectives every time.

- Anchor prices honestly: show the individual item total beside the combo price so the saving is checkable, never inflated.

- Frame outcomes as the customer's job done, not the product's features. Sell the hole, not the drill.

- Use urgency only from true facts: real stock counts or real campaign end dates. Manufactured urgency burns trust permanently.

- Give before asking: a useful tip, a free measurement, a genuine answer. Reciprocity outperforms pressure.

- Never use dark patterns: fake countdowns, invented stock scarcity, hidden charges or bait and switch pricing.

## Measure and iterate

- Track weekly: which keywords drove profile visits, which posts earned the most saves and shares, and which product pages gained search impressions.

- Change one variable per test: the keyword, the hook line or the hashtag set. Never change all three at once.

- Double down on the 20 percent of keywords and formats driving 80 percent of discovery. Cut the rest without sentiment.

- Recheck rankings monthly, since search behavior shifts with seasons and trends.
